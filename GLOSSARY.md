# Hungii

Hungii helps a person choose their next meal against their remaining nutrition, money, time, and taste preferences.

## Language

**Dish**:
A restaurant offering, such as a chicken bowl, with a particular portion and customization.

**Meal candidate**:
A proposed combination of dishes, quantities, and customizations intended for one eating occasion. Its nutrition refers to the portion the person intends to eat; its cost includes everything they would purchase.
_Avoid_: Product, dish when referring to a complete meal

**Meal opportunity**:
One eating occasion the person expects before the end of their current food day. It is a flexible planning assumption, including the next occasion, rather than a fixed breakfast, lunch, or dinner slot.

**Food day**:
The period over which a person tracks their daily intake and food spending. It has a visible boundary that can accommodate a late or irregular schedule.

**Daily targets**:
The person's chosen calorie and macro goals for a food day. A goal can be a range, a minimum, or a maximum; these are not interchangeable constraints.

**Remaining nutrition budget**:
Daily targets minus recorded consumption, with uncertainty preserved for estimated intake. Planned or ordered meals can reserve part of this budget without being counted as eaten.

**Food allowance**:
The amount the person has chosen to spend on food during a food day, including delivery fees and other checkout charges.

**Remaining food allowance**:
Food allowance minus committed spending and unresolved payment reservations. A refund restores allowance only when it is confirmed.

**Nutrition estimate**:
An approximation of calories and macros for a particular portion, accompanied by its source, assumptions, and uncertainty. An unknown nutrient is not zero.

**Verified cart total**:
The latest payable amount returned for the person's selected live cart, address, and payment conditions. It can change before checkout.
_Avoid_: Final price for an unverified search result

**Trade-off**:
A measurable benefit or compromise of choosing a meal candidate relative to the person's current plan and alternatives.

**Swipe pool**:
A small, varied set of meal candidates presented for the current decision. Passing removes a candidate from that decision; liking adds it to a shortlist.

**Finalist**:
A meal candidate the person has selected in the first round for their top-three shortlist. It remains a possible choice rather than a purchase commitment.

**Winner**:
The finalist revealed by the person's lucky-draw card choice, or explicitly chosen from the finalists, as the proposed next meal. It still requires review, live-cart verification, and separate order confirmation.

**Lucky draw**:
The second selection round, in which shortlisted finalists are turned face down and shuffled before the person picks a card. Each finalist has an equal chance of being revealed.

**Go-to meal**:
A meal the person has deliberately saved for future suggestions. Saving it does not guarantee future availability, price, or unchanged nutrition.
